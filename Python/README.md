# Fishing script v1
Prototype of a multifishing bot for Skyblock (Disclaimer: some of the script is vibe coded)

Even has a sort of app to change settings and stuff!
### Modes
* Default
* Treasure
* Trophy
* Magma Core
* Worm
* Strider

Allows deployable placement (Totem of C., SOS flare, Umberella, etc)

Has auto-lobby features if there's a problem with the game

Resets the bobber when stuck

### How to use?
1. You place the correct items in the right slot you have chosen (ex: if rod slot is 1, then put your rod in hotbar slot 1)
2. Aim at the water/lava (don't aim at the ground as it may break the bot)
3. Press the start button or mouse button 4 (usually its the thumb button for going back)
4. if you want to stop just press stop or mouse button 4
5. Select the correct mode of fishing
6. Tweak the constants (they have been tuned to my stats)
7. Start fishin, and always keep an eye on it

## Worm
### Used mainly for flaming worms in crystal hollows
* Find 1x1x1 lava that is greater than y=64
* For the funnel method dig out a small 1x1x1 hole to your left or right to allow the deployable placement
* Use a ranged weapon (Flaming Flay or Soul Whip)
* For the CHIMNEY method:
  * look straight down
  * No weapons needed to kill, only your fishing rod
  * One shot the Flaming Worms with your rod
    ###### Disables deployable placement
* For the FUNNEL method:
  * crouch to the very edge of the pit so that you can hit the worms.
  * One shot the worms with your ranged weapon
    ###### Allows deployable placement
